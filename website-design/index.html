YOUR FIRST STEP INTO AI

A website AI can read is your first step into AI.

Before any chatbot or automation, AI tools need clear facts about your business. We write your site so they can read it:

  • llms.txt: a plain-text summary of your business, written for AI tools
  • Structured data (schema): your company details in a format machines understand
  • Clear Q&A content: the questions customers ask, answered in your words

This site is an example. See our llms.txt.

FAQ

Website questions

How much does a website cost in Malaysia?

With us, Basic is RM399 and SEO + AEO/GEO is RM1,393 in total (Basic RM399 + RM994). Both include one language and your domain for the first year. More languages, extra pages or other custom work are confirmed by written quote. Prices in the market vary a lot with scope, so compare what each quote includes.

Does the website come with an admin login, so I can edit it myself?

Websites on the Basic and SEO + AEO/GEO packages don't come with an admin panel. That keeps the site faster and more secure, and easier for AI tools to read. To change something, just WhatsApp us. If you update often, you can choose maintenance at RM199/month. If you'd still rather log in and edit the text and images yourself, we can add an admin panel separately, by written quote.

What happens with the domain after the first year?

The first year of your domain is included. From the second year, domain renewal is billed separately. Your domain and website are registered in your name.

Can I add more languages?

Each package includes one language: English, 中文 or BM. More languages can be added, by written quote.

Do you guarantee the first page of Google?

No. Nobody controls how Google or AI tools rank websites. We build and set up exactly what's listed; ranking also depends on things like other sites linking to you, your Google Business Profile and customer reviews.

What's the difference between Basic and SEO + AEO/GEO?

Basic gives you a clean company website. SEO + AEO/GEO adds what search engines and AI tools read: page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, submission to Google and Bing, FAQ content, structured data and an indexing check report. It's a one-time setup. Optional maintenance: RM199/month.
