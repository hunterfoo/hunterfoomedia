Digital employees you can actually see.

HF Media Solution builds and runs AI “digital employees” for service businesses in Malaysia and Singapore. They're automation systems that do the repetitive work: sorting candidates, answering WhatsApp enquiries, preparing social posts. A person stays in charge of every decision that matters.

What we do, in plain terms

We work with small service businesses: recruitment agencies, clinics and beauty salons, property agents, tuition centres, and other owner-run teams that live on WhatsApp and Facebook.

Everything is delivered online. We map how you work over video call and WhatsApp, cut the steps that don't need to exist, then build the system on tools you already use, like WhatsApp, Google Sheets and Facebook. There's no shop, no walk-in office and nothing to install on site.

In daily use: these systems run every day for our client Sky Employment Pte Ltd, a licensed Singapore employment agency. See the case study below.

How an engagement works

  1. 1 · Setup

    A one-off project. We map your process, build the system, test it with you and train your team. Setup timeline and fees are agreed in a written quote before we start.

  2. 2 · Monthly operation

    An optional monthly fee. We keep the system running, adjust rules and message templates, and fix anything that breaks. Where we run paid ads for you, you get a daily ad performance report.

  3. 3 · You stay in control

    Your accounts, your data, your customers. You approve every outbound message template and every post before it goes live.

Case study: Sky Employment Pte Ltd

Sky Employment Pte Ltd is a Singapore employment agency (MOM EA licence 08C5793). HF Media Solution manages its recruitment marketing and operations, including its jobseeker channel, SG Jobs For Malaysians. The demos above are simplified versions of the systems we run for them.

What we manage

  • Facebook / Meta paid ads. Planning and running campaigns, with a daily ad performance report.
  • Facebook Page content. Daily job-info posts and hiring posters for the SG Jobs For Malaysians page and the Sky Employment Pte Ltd page.
  • WhatsApp Business. Replying to jobseekers, sending job details and the application form, following up, and labelling every chat.
  • WhatsApp Channel “SG Jobs For Malaysians”. Daily job updates for followers.
  • Google Drive & Sheets. The job order list, candidate registration through a Google Form, resume production and a resume index.
  • AI candidate-to-job matching. Each registered candidate is checked against open job orders and gets a list of recommended jobs.

By the numbers

  • 22candidates processed end-to-end through the automated pipeline: registration → resume → job recommendations
  • 49candidate-to-job recommendations generated

From internal records, as of 12 October 2026.

How we work

  1. Map

    A video call to see how you work today and where the hours go.

  2. Cut, then simplify

    We remove steps that shouldn't exist before automating anything.

  3. Build & test

    We build on your existing tools and test with real scenarios, with you watching.

  4. Run & improve

    Monthly operation: we monitor, adjust rules and report back.

Data & privacy

  • The demos on this page use made-up sample data only. No real candidate or customer data is shown or collected.
  • In client systems, data stays in the client's own accounts (for example their Google Sheets and WhatsApp Business). We only get the access needed to build and maintain the system.
  • Clients approve every outbound message template and every social post before it goes live. The system doesn't invent offers or reply outside the rules you set.
  • We handle personal data in line with Malaysia and Singapore personal data protection laws (PDPA).
