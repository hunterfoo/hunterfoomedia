What we do, in plain terms

We work with small service businesses: recruitment agencies, clinics and beauty salons, property agents, tuition centres, and other owner-run teams that live on WhatsApp and Facebook.

Everything is delivered online. We map how you work over video call and WhatsApp, cut the steps that don't need to exist, then build the system on tools you already use, like WhatsApp, Google Sheets and Facebook. There's no shop, no walk-in office and nothing to install on site.

In daily use: these systems run every day for our client Sky Employment Pte Ltd, a licensed Singapore employment agency. See the case study below.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a ChatGPT ad, and who sees it?

When someone asks ChatGPT a question, a short ad can appear below the answer, labelled Sponsored. It's matched to what the conversation is about, not to keywords. Ads are shown to people on the Free and Go plans, the largest group of ChatGPT users, so this is where an ad reaches the most people. They don't appear on sensitive topics. The example and demo above show what one can look like.

How much does it cost?

Scope and fees are agreed in a written quote before we start, for ChatGPT Ads and for the digital employees on this page. Your ad budget is separate: you decide how much to spend, and it's paid with your company card.

How soon will I see results?

We don't promise a timeline or a result. How ads perform depends on your offer, your page and the market. Where we run the ads for you, you get a daily ad performance report, so you always know how they're doing.

Do I get to approve the ads?

Yes. We write the ad copy and prepare the creative, but every ad is submitted only after you approve it. If something isn't right, ask for changes and we'll send a new draft for your approval.

Do I still need Google ads?

ChatGPT ads don't replace Google. Think of ChatGPT as another place your customers ask, beyond Google search. It runs alongside the channels you already use, so if Google ads bring you customers, keep them going.

How do you handle my data? What about PDPA?

We handle personal data in line with the personal data protection laws of Malaysia and Singapore (PDPA). Your accounts, your data and your customers stay yours: data stays in your own accounts, and we only get the access we need to set up and run the work.
