FAQ

Common questions

What is an AI agency?

An AI agency is a company that helps businesses put AI to work in their daily operations: setting it up, connecting it to how the business already runs, and keeping it working. It is different from only selling a software subscription.

How much does an AI agency cost in Malaysia?

It depends on the scope, so there is no single price. At Hunter Foo Media, website building is RM399 (Basic) and website maintenance + Google Business Profile management is RM499/month. Setting up your first main digital employee is free. These two prices are founding-client prices for the first 5 clients, in exchange for an honest testimonial, and will go up after 5 clients. Social media management, customized AI systems and the maintenance of your digital employees are quoted after we understand how your business works.

Will the AI send things to my customers without my approval?

No. Marketing AI publishes only after you approve, Customer Service AI hands complex cases to a person, and PA AI's drafted replies are sent only after you confirm.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or AI recommendations?

No. We tell you exactly what we build and set up; ranking and citation are up to search engines and AI tools.

Where are you, and do you serve Singapore?

We are based in Kuala Lumpur and serve businesses in Malaysia and Singapore, mostly online.

Is Hunter Foo Media a recruitment agency?

No. We don't charge job seekers or place candidates ourselves. See the About page for details.
